# Agent review rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Leave a short reason for every score.

| score | meaning                                               |
| ----: | ----------------------------------------------------- |
|     1 | absent, generic, broken, or actively fights the brief |
|     2 | weak and mostly incidental                            |
|     3 | workable but familiar or uneven                       |
|     4 | clear and intentional with minor weaknesses           |
|     5 | unusually coherent, specific, and memorable           |

## Dimensions

### discovery

Did the agent find and use the composition vocabulary without bespoke layout instructions?

### silhouette

Does the top-level arrangement differ from the default scene/card/grid pattern and from other outputs in the set?

### focal hierarchy

Can a reviewer identify what dominates and what supports it?

### relational meaning

Do overlap, echo, connect, anchor, frame, or interruption carry meaning rather than merely decorate?

### rhythm

Is repetition coherent, and does any interruption feel intentional?

### material identity

Does the output use HyvUI’s typography, token discipline, depth, material, and motion DNA without becoming a copy of the showcase?

### coherence

Do scale, spacing, contrast, and motion belong to the same art direction?

### control

Are manual decisions preserved, conflicts understandable, and adaptation inspectable?

### responsive survival

Does the visual idea reinterpret at narrow widths instead of collapsing into overflow or generic stacking?

### functional integrity

Do navigation, controls, focus, and core content still work?

### inspectability

Can an agent or human explain the nodes, relationships, seed, applied suggestions, and rejected suggestions?

## Reviewer prompts

- What is the visual idea in one sentence?
- What is the focal point?
- What is the counterweight or field?
- Where is the tension?
- What repeats, and what interrupts it?
- Which relationship is doing real work?
- What would remain recognizable in a blurred screenshot?
- What is the first generic choice you would remove?
